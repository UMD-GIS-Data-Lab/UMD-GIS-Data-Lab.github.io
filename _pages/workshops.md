At the **GIS and Data Service Lab** at the **University of Maryland Libraries**, we offer workshops spanning the topics of geospatial data, data science, and data visualization taught using a range of statistical software: ArcGIS, Python, SAS, R, NVivo, SQL, PowerBI, and Tableau.

Below is a list of workshops offered through our programming. Workshop titles link to basic materials, including slides and code. For **upcoming workshops** and **registration information**, please visit our calendar. [Library Calendar.](https://umd.libcal.com/calendar/events?cid=18601&t=d&d=0000-00-00&cal=18601&ct=65687&inc=0)
